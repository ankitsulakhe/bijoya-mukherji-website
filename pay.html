    Common questions

    How do online sessions work?

    Online sessions are held over a private video call from the comfort of your home. Once your slot is confirmed you'll receive the call link by email and WhatsApp.

    Where are in-person sessions held?

    The venue for Aura Cleansing Pranic Healing is shared with you when your slot is confirmed.

    Do I need any prior experience?

    Not at all. Whether you're new to somatic work or have done therapy before, we always start exactly where you are.

    What if I need to reschedule?

    You can reschedule any session with at least 24 hours' notice at no charge — just send a WhatsApp message.

    How do I pay?

    Payment details are shared along with your confirmed slot. Secure online payment (UPI, cards and netbanking) is coming soon.
